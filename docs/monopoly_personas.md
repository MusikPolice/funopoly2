# Monopoly AI Personas (Sesame Street Edition)

## 1. Decision Points in Monopoly

### Property Acquisition

-   Buy unowned property.
-   Decline and trigger auction.
-   Participate in auction with a bidding strategy.

### Cash Management

-   Maintain a cash buffer.
-   Reinvest aggressively.
-   Hoard cash to avoid liquidation.

### Building Houses/Hotels

-   Decide when and how many houses to build.
-   Upgrade to hotels.
-   Follow even-building rules.

### De-escalation

-   Sell houses evenly.
-   Mortgage properties.
-   Choose which assets to sacrifice first.

### Trading

-   Decide when to propose trades.
-   Evaluate fairness.
-   Value monopolies, cash, and blocking opponents.

## 3. Personas

### Oscar the Grouch --- The Slumlord

**Motivation:** Low-cost, high-volume returns.\
**Behavior:**\
- Buys cheap sets (Brown/Light Blue).\
- Low auction bids.\
- Builds exactly four houses; avoids hotels.\
- Minimal cash buffer.\
- Mortgages expensive properties first.\
- Rarely trades unless receiving multiple cheap properties.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Count von Count --- The High-Rent Maximizer

**Motivation:** Big rents, big numbers.\
**Behavior:**\
- Prioritizes Green/Dark Blue.\
- Aggressive in auctions for expensive sets.\
- Builds hotels ASAP.\
- Medium cash buffer.\
- Mortgages cheap properties first.\
- Trades to complete high-value sets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Elmo --- The Impulsive Novice

**Motivation:** Fun over strategy.\
**Behavior:**\
- Buys almost anything.\
- Random auction bidding.\
- Builds whenever possible.\
- Minimal cash buffer.\
- Random asset liquidation.\
- Accepts simple trades; rarely initiates.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Big Bird --- The Friendly Conservative

**Motivation:** Safety and stability.\
**Behavior:**\
- Prefers mid-priced properties.\
- Avoids auction wars.\
- Builds slowly and carefully.\
- High cash buffer.\
- Sells houses early to avoid risk.\
- Avoids trades that empower opponents.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Cookie Monster --- The Liquidity Gambler

**Motivation:** Spend cash fast, grow assets.\
**Behavior:**\
- Buys nearly everything; loves railroads.\
- Overbids in auctions.\
- Builds aggressively.\
- Keeps almost no cash.\
- Mortgages widely; only sells houses as last resort.\
- Trades impulsively to complete sets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Bert --- The Calculating Planner

**Motivation:** Mathematical optimization.\
**Behavior:**\
- Targets Orange/Red due to ROI.\
- Sets strict max bids.\
- Builds three houses first (rent efficiency).\
- Keeps a safe cash buffer.\
- Liquidates low-ROI properties first.\
- Trades only for positive expected value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Ernie --- The Chaotic Opportunist

**Motivation:** Disruption and unpredictability.\
**Behavior:**\
- Buys strategically to block opponents.\
- Chaotic auction strategy.\
- Builds intimidating uneven improvements.\
- Variable cash behavior.\
- Emotional choices in liquidation.\
- Proposes chaotic, multi-way trades.
