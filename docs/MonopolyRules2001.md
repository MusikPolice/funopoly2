# Monopoly -- Quick Rules

## Top Tips

-   To keep the game short, **don't use house rules**.
-   Always **auction** when a player doesn't want to buy a property.
-   Never **loan money** or make "no-rent" deals.
-   Never put cash in the center of the board; **Free Parking gives no bonus**.

# Help! I Can't Pay!

### Try to raise money:

If you owe money, attempt to pay by selling buildings and/or mortgaging
properties.

### Selling Buildings

-   Sell **Hotels** to the Bank for half cost → receive 4 Houses back.
-   Sell **Houses** to the Bank for half cost.
-   Houses must be sold **evenly** across the color set.

### Mortgaging Property

1.  Sell all buildings in the color set first.
2.  Turn the Title Deed face down and collect the **mortgage value**.
3.  To unmortgage, pay **mortgage +10%**.
4.  Rent cannot be collected on mortgaged properties.
5.  Increased rent for **unmortgaged** properties in a complete color set still
    applies.

### Still in debt?

You're **bankrupt**.

#### If you owe another player:

-   Give them your mortgaged properties and any Get Out of Jail Free cards.
-   They must immediately either:
    -   Pay the unmortgage cost, **or**
    -   Pay 10% of mortgage value to keep it mortgaged.

#### If you owe the Bank:

-   Return all properties; mortgages are canceled.
-   All properties are auctioned.
-   Return any Get Out of Jail Free cards to their decks.

**The game continues until one player remains.**
That player wins.

# Buildings, Deals & Trades

## Deals & Trades

-   You can buy, sell, or trade property with others anytime.
-   Buildings **must be sold to the Bank** before trading streets.
-   You can't trade buildings themselves.
-   Trades may include **cash, property, or Get Out of Jail Free cards**.
-   Mortgaged properties may be traded; new owner must:
    -   Pay the unmortgage cost, **or**
    -   Pay 10% of mortgage value to keep it mortgaged.

## Building Houses

-   Once you own a full color set, you can start buying Houses immediately - there's no need to wait for your next turn.
-   Build **evenly** across the set.
-   Max **4 Houses** per street.

### Not enough buildings?

If multiple players want the last House/Hotel, the Banker **auctions** it starting at **$10**, with raises as small as **$1**. Proceeds go to the bank.

### No buildings left?

You must wait for another player to sell some back.

## Building Hotels

-   After 4 Houses are on **each** street in a color set, you can upgrade to a Hotel.
-   Pay the Hotel cost, return 4 Houses, place 1 Hotel.
-   Only **1 Hotel per street**.
-   You cannot build if any property in the color set is mortgaged.

# Set It Up!

## Banker

Choose someone to be the Banker. They manage: 
- The Bank's money
- Houses & Hotels
- Title Deed cards
- Auctions

(The Banker may still play but must keep Bank money separate from their own.)

## Preparing the Game

1.  Shuffle Community Chest cards face down.
2.  Shuffle Chance cards face down.
3.  Each player chooses a token and places it on **GO**.
4.  Place the dice by the board.
5.  Banker gives each player:

```text
  Bill   Quantity
  ------ ----------
  $500   2
  $100   4
  $50    1
  $20    1
  $10    2
  $5     1
  $1     5
```

**Total: $1500**

# Play!

## How to Win

Buy properties, collect rent, and be the last player not bankrupt.

## Who Goes First?

Everyone rolls both dice. Highest roll starts.

## Your Turn

1.  Roll both dice.
2.  Move your token clockwise.
3.  Follow the rules of the space where you land (see Board Spaces).
4.  If you roll **doubles**, take another turn.
    -   Three consecutive doubles → **go to Jail immediately** (don't
        finish the third turn).
5.  End your turn; pass dice left.

# The Board Spaces

## Properties

You'll encounter **streets**, **railroads**, and **utilities**.

### Unowned Properties

You must **buy or auction**.

**Buy:** Pay the price & take the Title Deed.
**Don't want to buy?** Banker auctions it starting at **$10**, increments of **$1**.

### Collect Color Sets

Owning all streets in a color set allows you to:
- **Double the rent**
- Build Houses and Hotels

### Owned Properties

Land on someone's property?
You must pay rent *if they ask before the next player rolls*.

### Rent Rules

-   **Streets:** Use the rent value on the Title Deed.
-   **Railroads:** Rent depends on number owned.
-   **Utilities:** Roll dice → rent is 4× or 10× your roll depending on how many utilities the owner has.

# Action Spaces

### GO

Collect **$200** when you pass or land on GO.

### Just Visiting

Landing here does nothing.

### Chance / Community Chest

Draw the top card and do what it says; return it to the bottom of the
deck.

### Go to Jail

Move directly to Jail.
Do **not** collect $200.
Turn ends.
While in Jail, you may continue to:
- Collect rent
- Bid in auctions
- Buy buildings
- Mortgage
- Trade

### Income Tax / Luxury Tax

Pay the amount shown.

### Free Parking

Nothing happens.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Getting Out of Jail

You have **3 options**: 
1. Pay **$50** at the start of your turn.
2. Use a Get Out of Jail Free card (or buy one).
3. Roll **doubles** within 3 attempts.
   - If still no doubles by the third attempt, pay **$50** and move using that roll.
